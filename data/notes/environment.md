Retrieved 2026-10-31. Raw files: `data/raw/environment/`. Processed: `data/processed/`. Scripts: `scripts/env_0*.py`.
Everything labelled ESTIMATE below is a model output or assumption, not a measurement. JHU subscription sources (ProQuest, Statista) were NOT used; `data/private/` holds nothing from this domain.

## 2. Facility screening model (`scripts/env_04_screening_model.py`)

Run `python3 -I scripts/env_04_screening_model.py facilities.csv out.csv`. Input columns: facility_id, state, sqft, optional lat, lon, year. Output carries low / central / high columns plus a flag "ESTIMATE".

| Component | Formula | Low / central / high | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electricity | sqft x kWh/sqft | 3.4 / 5.8 / 12.6 kWh per sq ft | CBECS 2018 warehouse median (C14), warehouse mean (C14; cross-check 95 billion kWh / 16,335 million sq ft = 5.82), all-commercial mean 12.6 as a proxy for a conditioned, automated building (ASSUMPTION for the high case) |
| Grid CO2e | kWh/1000 x eGRID subregion CO2e lb/MWh x 0.4536/1000 | factor varies by subregion and year (2023 range: see `egrid_subregion_rates_2018_2023.csv`) | eGRID. Pre-2018 years scale the 2023 subregion value by US average ratio (ESTIMATE). Years after 2023 use 2023. |
| Natural gas (heating) | sqft x kBtu/sqft x 53.06 kg/mmBtu | 5.7 / 11.38 / 22.8 kBtu per sq ft | 11.38 derived from CBECS C1 (199 TBtu gas / 17,483 million sq ft); factor from EPA Hub Table 1; the 0.5x and 2x range is an ASSUMPTION (no climate or size breakdown for warehouses) |
| Trucks (CO2, NOx, PM2.5) | NOT ESTIMATED | | **Not estimated: no primary trip-rate source.** The earlier version (trip rates 0.53 / 0.717 / 1.37 per 1,000 sq ft per day) used values read from search-result snippets of secondary documents, and the miles, speed and multiplier inputs were unsourced assumptions. A single search for a primary source (2026-10-31) found the South Coast AQMD High Cube Warehouse Truck Trip Study (https://www.aqmd.gov/home/rules-compliance/ceqa/air-quality-analysis-handbook/high-cube-warehouse): CE-CERT memo of 7 Aug 2014, p.2 (simple-average truck trips per 1,000 sq ft per day: 0.40 without cold storage, 1.10 cold storage only; percentiles 0.33 to 0.98 without cold storage) and the AQMD summary slides of 25 Jul 2014 (composite 0.50). That study removed e-commerce and parcel warehouses as outliers with substantially higher trip rates, so it does not describe fulfillment centers; the CE-CERT memo also notes 2013/14 truck counts were 140% above 2008 counts at eight repeat sites. The Rule 2305 (WAIRE) annual reports contain no per-building rate, and no ITE high-cube fulfillment rate was found in a primary document (only in a San Diego APCD presentation, a secondary source). Per-site truck columns and truck totals were removed from the pages and data files. |
| Delivery vans (last mile) | not modelled | | No citable per-station van count or route length. Use Amazon Scope 1 (section 3) as a top-down total instead. |

### Worked sensitivity (one 1,000,000 sq ft building, 2023 factors; file `environment_screening_model_reference_buildings.csv`)
Virginia (SRVC, 596.3 lb CO2e/MWh): grid electricity 920 / 1,569 / 3,408 t CO2e per year; gas 302 / 604 / 1,208 t.
California (CAMX, 430.0 lb/MWh): grid 663 / 1,131 / 2,458 t. Ohio (RFCW, 916.1 lb/MWh): grid 1,413 / 2,410 / 5,236 t.
Reading: electricity intensity moves electricity emissions about 3.7-fold between low and high, and the grid factor moves them about 2x between regions. Trucks and vans are not modelled.
Facility-level validation is not possible: Amazon publishes no facility electricity, fuel or trip data, and its reports give no total kWh that could calibrate kWh per sq ft (a top-down check was not available).

## 3. Amazon corporate figures (global; see file 1 for all vintages)
Latest vintage (2025 report, p46): Scope 1 5.76 Mt (2019) rising to 15.37 Mt (2025); Scope 2 market-based 5.50 to 3.74 Mt (up 34% in 2025); Scope 3 39.91 to 61.74 Mt; total 51.17 to 80.85 Mt; carbon intensity 182.4 to 112.8 g CO2e per $ revenue (up 3% in 2025). Scope 1 is mainly transportation fuel (2023 report p11); fossil fuels were 15.00 of 15.37 Mt in 2025. Electric delivery vans: 19K (2023) to 31.4K (2024) to 52.7K (2025; 37.4K US, 4,400 India, 10,900 Europe; Rivian 38,100). Single-use plastic packaging: 88,698 t (2023), 74,137 t (2024), 74,073 t (2025) globally; share of North American shipments containing plastic 65%, 37%, 27% (2025 report p22).
Methodology (Amazon carbon-methodology.pdf, saved): location-based factors use EPA eGRID for the US; Scope 2 is reported market-based. Because Amazon reports market-based Scope 2 with 100% renewable matching, the facility screening model uses location-based eGRID factors, which will exceed the corporate market-based numbers by design.

## 4. Demographics near facilities (METHOD ONLY, integrator joins)
`scripts/env_06_census_tract_method.py` documents and implements step 1 (coordinates to 2020 tract GEOID, tested on 33 sites, 100% matched). Steps 2-3 are documented but NOT run: ACS 5-year tract tables (B01003, B03002, B19013, B17001, B25003, B23025, B08201) via api.census.gov need a free key (the call returned "missing key"); the 2024 ACS 5-year endpoint exists. Neighbourhood method: tracts whose Gazetteer centroid lies within 1.6 km and 5 km, population-weighted shares computed from summed numerators and denominators, never an average of tract percentages. Report margins of error; flag tracts under 500 people (industrial tracts are often nearly empty); median income only for the containing tract. EPA EJScreen was not used.

## 5. Air permits and regulation
- ICIS-Air (file 8): 33 Amazon logistics sites with air-permit records, 26 Minor, 3 Synthetic Minor, 4 with no classification in ICIS; many carry MACT/NSPS flags, which is consistent with (not proof of) emergency diesel engines. Example: Amazon fulfillment sites in Oklahoma City/Tulsa (OKC1, OKC2, OKC5, TUL2, TUL5, DOK4, DOK6, SOKC1), Aurora CO (DEN5, DEN8), Kearny NJ (DJZ4). 10 of the 33 have at least one recorded evaluation (7 a full compliance evaluation) and none shows a recent violation count in this extract. Data centers (54 operating) dominate the Amazon-named permit list, mostly Virginia (generators).
- CVG (Hebron, KY): no air permit under an Amazon name was found for the air hub. A Kentucky DAQ draft permit F-25-027 is for "Amazon Data Services" at Florence KY (a data center; diesel emergency generators), and the airport authority holds its own permit (F-23-042). The Kentucky documents could not be downloaded here (server returned HTML), so nothing is quoted from them.
- San Bernardino International Airport (KSBD): 9th Circuit amended opinion (11 Oct 2022) denied petitions for review of the FAA's finding of no significant impact for a 660,000 sq ft Amazon air cargo facility (the 660,000 sq ft figure came from a search-result summary; the string does not appear in the saved opinion text, so treat it as unverified) and rejected challenges to the truck-trip emission method. No ARB/SCAQMD permit data for the hub were obtained.
- South Coast AQMD Rule 2305 (Warehouse Indirect Source Rule, WAIRE): applies to operators of warehouses of 100,000 sq ft or more; operators earn points set by annual truck trips (larger trucks counted 2.5x). 4th Annual Report (October 2026, p ES-1/ES-2): 4,051 potentially affected warehouses, 4,848 annual reports filed through 31 Aug 2026 for 2022-2025, about 1.71 tons per day NOx and 0.047 tons per day DPM reductions in 2025, $79.3 million mitigation fees collected. Operator-level data (Amazon) are not published; they would need a public-records request to SCAQMD.

## 6. Gaps (be honest)
1. No facility-level measured energy, fuel, truck or van data exist publicly; everything facility-level is a model.
2. Truck emissions are not estimated (no primary trip-rate source for fulfillment centers; see section 2). The gas range (0.5x to 2x) is an unsourced assumption.
3. NOx and PM per mile come from a per-hour rate and an assumed speed; MOVES was not run, no tire/brake PM, no idling.
4. Renewable share 2020 unverified; 2019 and 2021 secondary; 2019-2022 Amazon report PDFs not obtained; 2024-report carbon intensity values missing.
5. ACS tract data not pulled (API key needed); EJScreen not used; no pollution-burden indices.
6. NLCD only through 2021 (newer sites read zero); no parcel or footprint areas, so land area and impervious acreage per facility are not estimated (a footprint rule such as sq ft divided by floors was not applied because no citable floors or floor-area ratio was found).
7. eGRID subregion and state history before 2018 not parsed; 2008, 2011, 2013, 2015, 2017 national values missing; 2018 state table has 51 rows.
8. Air hub permits (CVG, KSBD/Riverside-San Bernardino) not found or not downloadable; WISR compliance by operator unavailable.
9. JHU subscription sources were not used (Chrome login state not checked).
